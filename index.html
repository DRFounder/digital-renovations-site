Flagship initiative · In development

Veteran Companion

Veteran-aware support, built with mission boundaries.

Built by a Veteran, for Veterans.

Veteran Companion is an emerging AI safety companion designed to help veterans orient, reflect, and connect with appropriate human support while preserving agency and privacy.

It is not a therapist, doctor, emergency service, law-enforcement tool, or replacement for the people and professionals in a veteran’s life.

About the founder

Experience behind the mission.

Digital Renovations is led by a South Dakota veteran whose background spans technology, public safety, emergency management, crisis response, and organizational leadership. His experience includes service as a chief of police, work in emergency medical response, participation on the FEMA Region VIII Threat Analysis Team, and instruction in Critical Incident Stress Management. He holds degrees in computer science and criminal justice.

That combination of lived, technical, and operational experience informs the mission and safety architecture of Veteran Companion. The project is being developed with a clear understanding that responsible systems require multidisciplinary participation—including veteran, technical, clinical, research, privacy, and security expertise.
